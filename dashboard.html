⚡ Getting Started & Testing Guide

Welcome to Chatmagal, a multi-tenant WhatsApp Business platform. Your local stack is fully connected. Follow these instructions to perform integration testing:

1. Test Inbound Messages (Live Inbox) Send a standard WhatsApp text to your official Meta developer phone number. It routes through localtunnel, hits your webhook, and immediately pops up in the Live Shared Inbox tab.
2. Outbound Broadcasts Open the Broadcast Campaigns tab, click **Launch Campaign**, and enter your phone number. The server automatically batches contacts in groups of 50 and handles background delivery via BullMQ and Redis.
3. Real-Time Chat Console Open the Live Shared Inbox tab, select your contact, type a message, and hit send! It will immediately push a live text reply to your phone and bill the Workspace balance.
