Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 22, 2026

1. INTRODUCTION & SCOPE

Welcome to Chatmagal ("we," "our," or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, process, and secure your data when you use the Chatmagal platform and our WhatsApp Business API integration services.

Our Role: Under applicable data protection frameworks, Chatmagal acts as a Data Controller concerning our direct clients' (your) account information (e.g., billing details, login credentials). We act as a Data Processor concerning the end-user messaging data routed through our platform on your behalf.

We are strictly committed to adhering to the Nigerian Data Protection Act (NDPA), global privacy standards including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

2. DATA WE COLLECT

To provide our services effectively, we collect the following categories of information:

  • Account Information (Data Controller): When you register as a client, we collect your name, email address, billing details, and company name.
  • Messaging Data (Data Processor): As a routing platform, we process end-user phone numbers, WhatsApp profile display names, timestamps, text message content, and media files as they flow through the WhatsApp Business API.
  • Technical Logs: We automatically log IP addresses, browser types, device information, and system activity logs for security monitoring, debugging, and service optimization.

3. HOW WE USE THE DATA

We process the collected data strictly to ensure the delivery and maintenance of our core services:

  • Routing messages accurately between your dashboard and the WhatsApp Business API.
  • Providing analytics, delivery statuses, and dashboard metrics.
  • Handling customer support inquiries and managing your billing cycles.
  • Ensuring platform security and preventing fraudulent activities.
Strict Non-Compromise Clause
We respect confidentiality. We DO NOT sell user data to data brokers. We DO NOT use message content for targeted advertising. We DO NOT use any messaging data to train unauthorized AI models or LLMs without explicit, documented consent from the data owner.

4. THIRD-PARTY DISCLOSURES & INFRASTRUCTURE

Chatmagal's architecture relies on secure third-party infrastructure to operate:

  • Meta Platforms, Inc.: As a WhatsApp Business API service provider, messaging data securely transits through Meta's servers. Your use of Chatmagal is inherently subject to Meta's WhatsApp Business Terms of Service.
  • Cloud Infrastructure: Your data is securely hosted and processed on our self-hosted VPS infrastructure and managed using Google Cloud (Firestore/Firebase) for real-time database configurations and secure authentication.

We only share data with these trusted infrastructure partners to the extent required to execute the messaging service.

5. DATA RETENTION & SECURITY

Data Retention: Message logs and media transiting through our platform are retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill the service or as explicitly configured by you (the client) in your dashboard. Once the retention period expires or upon request, these logs are permanently purged from our active databases.

Security Measures: We employ robust security standards, including:

  • SSL/TLS Encryption: All data in transit between your browser, our servers, and Meta's API is fully encrypted.
  • Encryption at Rest: Sensitive database records stored in Google Cloud Firestore are encrypted at rest.
  • Server Auditing: Regular server maintenance, patching, and access-control reviews on our self-hosted VPS resources.

6. DATA SUBJECT RIGHTS

Under the NDPA, GDPR, and other applicable laws, individuals have rights concerning their personal data:

  • For Direct Clients: You may access, modify, export, or delete your account information directly from the platform Settings dashboard, or by contacting our support team.
  • For End-Users (Consumers): If you are an end-user interacting with a business powered by Chatmagal over WhatsApp and wish to exercise your rights (such as data deletion or access), you must contact that specific business directly. As a Data Processor, Chatmagal will promptly facilitate and execute data deletion or export requests upstream when initiated by our clients.
